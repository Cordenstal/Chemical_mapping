# CDR Cleaning Practice Notebook

- Canonical source path: `jupyter notebook 1.ipynb`
- Source type: Jupyter notebook scaffold
- Why it matters: this notebook is the working space for practicing structured CSV cleaning, with explicit logging and step-by-step validation.
- Key points:
  - Loads the canonical CDR CSV from `cleaned data/`.
  - Keeps the raw dataframe separate from the working copy.
  - Includes progress logging and a session log for debugging long or hung runs.
  - Provides exercises for column cleanup, text normalization, type conversion, and export.
  - Builds ontology seed tables for chemicals and companies from the wide source sheet.
  - Normalizes company names first, chemical IDs next, and preserves `source_row_id` for every derived fact.
  - Unpivots wide quantity columns into long-form facts and splits physical forms into atomic rows.
  - Includes a pre-table normalization checklist for encoding, keys, sentinels, provenance, and atomic field handling.
  - Starts canonical company, chemical, activity, quantity, and physical-form tables with normalized keys.
  - Keeps raw quantity values and a parsed numeric helper column so text ranges do not get lost.
