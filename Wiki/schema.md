# Wiki Schema

Page types:
- `sources/`: canonical repo files, policy documents, or other immutable references.
- `documents/`: repository-facing docs, plans, and working notes.
- `scripts/`: automation, setup, and maintenance notes for executable files.

Every dedicated page should include:
- Canonical source path
- Source type
- Why it matters
- Key points
- Update triggers
- Last reviewed date

Maintenance rules:
- Keep pages short and specific.
- Use `index.md` as the navigation catalog.
- Keep `log.md` append-only.
